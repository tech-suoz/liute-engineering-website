FAQ

Quick answers.

What types of electrical engineering projects does LIUTE support?

LIUTE supports electrical projects across industrial, commercial, energy and infrastructure facilities, covering LV and MV systems, substations and transformers, MCCs, control panels, cabling, earthing and lightning protection, and lighting.

Can LIUTE support procurement and industrial material supply?

Yes. Supply covers electrical, instrumentation, telecoms, mechanical and pipeline categories, with vendor sourcing, technical compliance checks, cost comparison, inspection and delivery coordination.

Does the scope include testing and commissioning?

Yes. LIUTE tests and commissions systems and hands them over with documentation, test records and as-built information.

What should I include when requesting a proposal?

A description of the scope, the type of facility, the systems involved, any applicable standards or specifications, and your target timeline. Drawings or specifications help; send them after first contact.
