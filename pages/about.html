Overview

Practical engineering, disciplined execution.

LIUTE Engineering Solutions delivers integrated services spanning electrical procurement, skilled manpower supply, construction, installation, testing and commissioning. Our solutions are engineered to ensure safety, reliability and optimal performance under real operating conditions.

Driven by practical engineering expertise, disciplined project execution and a deep understanding of client requirements, we are committed to delivering projects that meet the highest standards of quality while maintaining strict control over cost and schedule.

Safety, quality and compliance

Safety planned into every task.

Our safety operations are built around risk-based electrical engineering, disciplined site control and continuous hazard mitigation throughout the project lifecycle.

Ask about our standards
  • Electrical risk identification tied directly to project scope
  • Task-specific hazard analysis for installation, testing and commissioning
  • Lock-out/tag-out (LOTO) planning for energised and de-energised works
  • Method statements aligned with voltage levels, work environments and system criticality
  • Applicable IEC, IEEE and BS standards, and client-specific standards where required
  • Structured QA/QC with insulation and earth resistance testing
  • Complete documentation, test records and as-built information at handover

FAQ

Something else? Ask an engineer.

Does every project follow the same process?

The sequence above reflects our documented scope of work. Each project is planned around its own requirements.

How do I start a project with LIUTE?

Send a short description of your scope through the request-a-quote form, or call or email us. An engineer will review it and come back with questions or next steps.

What standards does LIUTE work to?

Work is executed to applicable IEC, IEEE, BS and client-specific standards, supported by structured QA/QC and safety practices. Ask us for specifics on your project.

Do you provide documentation at handover?

Yes. Systems are delivered with complete documentation, test records and as-built information.

Can LIUTE supply manpower for my own project?

Yes, skilled electrical manpower is one of our core services. Contact us with the roles and duration you need.

Does LIUTE hold specific certifications?

Our profile states work to IEC, IEEE, BS and client-specific standards with QA/QC and safety practices. For certification details, please contact us directly.
