Overview

Earthing and lightning protection

Earth pits, earth rods, earth resistance testing and lightning air terminals.

Earthing and lightning protection systems are part of LIUTE's electrical project delivery scope, with documented work on earth pit excavation, earth rod installation, earth resistance testing and lightning air terminal assembly.

Each project is executed with strict adherence to applicable IEC, IEEE, BS and client-specific standards, supported by structured QA/QC and safety practices. Earthing systems are also part of our electrical materials supply.

Scope covered

  • Earth pit excavation
  • Earth rod installation
  • Earth resistance testing
  • Lightning air terminal assembly
  • Earthing systems supply
  • Test records at handover

How we deliver it

From planning to handover.

01 / 05

Plan

Earthing works are planned against the project scope, including excavation and installation activities. Electrical risk identification is tied directly to project scope. Task-specific hazard analysis covers installation, testing and commissioning activities, lock-out/tag-out (LOTO) planning covers energised and de-energised works, and method statements are aligned with voltage levels, work environments and system criticality.

02 / 05

Procure

Earthing systems are part of our electrical materials and equipment supply. Equipment and material selection is based on performance and lifecycle value. Vendors are technically evaluated and compliance is verified, with BOQ development and cost alignment, cost comparison, inspection and delivery coordination.

03 / 05

Install

Documented activities include excavation of an earth pit, driving of an earth rod into the ground and assembling of a lightning air terminal.

04 / 05

Test

Earth resistance testing is a documented test activity. Our commissioning philosophy emphasises functional integrity and system reliability, early fault identification and resolution, and a smooth transition from construction to operation.

05 / 05

Hand over

All systems are delivered with complete documentation, test records and as-built information to support safe and efficient operation, with ongoing support beyond project handover.
