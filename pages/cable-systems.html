Overview

Cable systems and termination

Cable containment, routing, preparation, glanding, termination, jointing and protection.

Cable containment systems, routing, termination and protection are core parts of LIUTE's electrical delivery, supported by cable pulling and management, cable preparation, glanding, termination and jointing.

Each project is executed with strict adherence to applicable IEC, IEEE, BS and client-specific standards, supported by structured QA/QC and safety practices. Underground cable works and cable system management are carried out by skilled teams under supervision.

Scope covered

  • Cable containment and routing
  • Cable pulling and cable management
  • Cable preparation, glanding and termination
  • Cable jointing
  • Underground cable-related works
  • Insulation resistance testing

How we deliver it

From planning to handover.

01 / 05

Plan

Cable routes, pulling preparation and jointing works are planned with task-specific hazard analysis. Electrical risk identification is tied directly to project scope. Task-specific hazard analysis covers installation, testing and commissioning activities, lock-out/tag-out (LOTO) planning covers energised and de-energised works, and method statements are aligned with voltage levels, work environments and system criticality.

02 / 05

Procure

Cables, containment and installation consumables are part of our electrical supply scope; delivery of power cable from the factory is a documented activity. Equipment and material selection is based on performance and lifecycle value. Vendors are technically evaluated and compliance is verified, with BOQ development and cost alignment, cost comparison, inspection and delivery coordination.

03 / 05

Install

Documented activities include cable preparation for jointing, cable pulling preparation and cable pulling works, concrete slabbing of underground cables, cable jointing, cable glanding and termination and cable system management.

04 / 05

Test

Cable IR testing after glanding is a documented test activity. Our commissioning philosophy emphasises functional integrity and system reliability, early fault identification and resolution, and a smooth transition from construction to operation.

05 / 05

Hand over

All systems are delivered with complete documentation, test records and as-built information to support safe and efficient operation, with ongoing support beyond project handover.
