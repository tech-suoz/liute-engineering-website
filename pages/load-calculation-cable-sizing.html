The service

Sound calculations behind safe, correctly sized electrical systems.

We calculate electrical loads, size cables and assess power systems so installations are sized for the duty they carry, with documented calculations that can be reviewed and traced.

Calculations follow applicable IEC, IEEE, BS and client-specific standards. Tell us which apply to your project.
