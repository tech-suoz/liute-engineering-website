Overview

Energy and electrical infrastructure

LIUTE serves the energy sector within its electrical project delivery scope, covering substations, transformers and power distribution networks. This page describes the scope of work, not named clients.

Transformers

Testing and commissioning of a power distribution transformer.

Distribution panels

Installation and commissioning of voltage monitoring and power distribution panels.

Cable jointing

Cable jointing works and cable termination.

Earthing

Earth pit, earth rod and earth resistance testing.

Lightning protection

Assembling of lightning air terminals.

Documentation

Test records and as-built information at handover.

Does LIUTE work on substations and transformers?

Yes. Substations, transformers and power distribution networks are part of our scope, and transformer commissioning is a documented activity.

Are test records provided?

Yes. Complete documentation, test records and as-built information are delivered at handover.

Does LIUTE name its clients?

Not on this website. Sector pages describe the scope of work, and client details are shared directly where appropriate.
